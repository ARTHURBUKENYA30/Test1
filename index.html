OUR HERITAGE

Where Uganda's printed history comes alive

The Uganda Printing & Publishing Museum celebrates more than a century of printing, publishing, journalism, design and information preservation in Uganda.

From early government publications and newspapers to modern digital production, printing has played a major role in documenting Uganda's political, social, cultural and economic development.

The museum provides a space where visitors can explore historic printing equipment, publications, photographs, documents and the people behind Uganda's print heritage.

OUR TIMELINE

A Century of Printing in Uganda

1902

The Government Printer

Uganda's printing history reaches a major milestone with the establishment of the Government Printer during the colonial period.

1962

Independence Era

Following independence, government printing continued to support the production of official documents, publications and national information.

1992

Uganda Printing & Publishing Corporation

The Uganda Printing and Publishing Corporation was established as a public enterprise.

2000s

Digital Transformation

Printing and publishing increasingly adopted digital workflows, modern production systems and electronic publishing technologies.

TODAY

Preserving the Future

The museum brings together Uganda's printing heritage and the technologies shaping the future of publishing.
